Behind The Work

Shaping Emotion Through Visual Craft

"Editing is never just cutting footage together; it is finding the hidden heartbeat of the story and making the viewer feel every second of it."

I am Shushil Bastola, a multidisciplinary creative based in the scenic city of Pokhara, Nepal. My creative journey began with a curiosity for how moving images, sound, and graphic composition shape human emotion and tell stories that resonate.

Graduating from Tribhuvan University, I blend creative intuition with technical discipline. Rather than confining myself to a single narrow tool, I operate across the entire visual pipeline: designing brand graphics in Photoshop & Illustrator, bringing them to life through After Effects motion design, and editing cinematic narratives in Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve.

How I Work

The Creative Workflow

A disciplined 5-step approach ensuring clarity, creative alignment, and polished delivery for every single project.

01

Understand & Brief

We review your goals, target audience, reference style, and creative guidelines to define clear benchmarks.

02

Ingest & Structure

Organizing raw footage or creative assets, creating selects, selecting soundtrack directions, and outlining pacing.

03

The Core Assembly

Building the visual spine: story cuts, rhythm-matched transitions, initial typography hierarchy, and narrative flow.

04

Motion, Color & Sound

Infusing magic with DaVinci color grading, motion graphics title cards, sound foley, and soundscape design.

05

Refine & Deliver

Incorporating constructive feedback rounds, finishing master exports in target formats (4K, 1080p, 9:16).
