Who I Am

From Pokhara to Digital Canvases

"Good editing is invisible when it needs to be, and electrifying when the moment calls for it."

Born and based in the natural visual sanctuary of Pokhara, Nepal, I have spent years honing my craft at the crossroads of visual storytelling and digital craftsmanship. The surrounding lakes, rolling hills, and majestic Himalayan backdrop instilled in me an appreciation for light, atmosphere, and natural pacing.

As a Tribhuvan University graduate, I approach video editing and graphic design with both analytical rigor and creative flexibility. My philosophy is grounded in empathy for the viewer: understanding how long their eye lingers on an image, what audio cues invoke emotional resonance, and how colors evoke psychological connection.

Whether cutting a travel documentary, synchronizing beats for an indie music video, crafting kinetic titles in After Effects, or drafting clean corporate identities in Illustrator, I treat every project with absolute dedication to craftsmanship.

My Triple-Discipline Advantage

Video Editing & Color Science:

Focusing on narrative flow, foley immersion, and cinematic color grading in DaVinci Resolve & Premiere Pro.

Motion Graphics & Typography:

Designing custom kinetic sequences, logo reveals, and HUD/screen assets in After Effects.

Graphic Design & Brand Aesthetics:

Creating high-contrast typographic posters, digital covers, and brand visual identities in Photoshop and Illustrator.
